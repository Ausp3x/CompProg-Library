# 04 Graphs — inventory

Scope: combinatorial graph and tree algorithms over the canonical `Graph`/`CsrGraph`/`DenseGraph` types with stable edge and arc IDs: traversal, shortest paths, spanning trees, connectivity, flows, matchings, decompositions, counting adapters and certificates. Data Structures owns the DSU, link-cut, Euler-tour-tree and top-tree engines that rows here adapt; Mathematics owns determinant, permanent, polynomial and generating-function kernels used by graph counting; Miscellaneous owns small-to-large/DSU-on-tree merging, generic cycle finding and exchange-rule scheduling; Geometry owns Manhattan MST and geometric graphs. Contracts and ownership rules live in [00-notes.md](Docs/00-notes.md), the source ledger in [00-sources.md](Docs/00-sources.md).
