# 04 Graphs — inventory

Scope: combinatorial graph and tree algorithms over the canonical `Graph`/`CsrGraph`/`DenseGraph` types with stable edge and arc IDs: traversal, shortest paths, spanning trees, connectivity, flows, matchings, decompositions, counting adapters and certificates. Data Structures owns the DSU, link-cut, Euler-tour-tree and top-tree engines that rows here adapt; Mathematics owns determinant, permanent, polynomial and generating-function kernels used by graph counting; Miscellaneous owns small-to-large/DSU-on-tree merging, generic cycle finding and exchange-rule scheduling; Geometry owns Manhattan MST and geometric graphs. Walks versus simple paths, cardinality versus weighted matching, given embedding versus planarity recognition, and offline versus online dynamic connectivity are separate rows. Contracts live in [80-notes.md](80-notes.md), the source ledger in [81-sources.md](81-sources.md).

## Notes

- DSU, link-cut, Euler-tour-tree and top-tree engines are Data Structures rows 01, 09, 37, 38, 39; `04-dsu.hpp` and `51-lct.hpp` are graph-facing adapters only.
- Small-to-large/DSU-on-tree merging is Miscellaneous `15-smalltolarge.hpp`; `14-tree_algorithms.hpp` supplies its Euler-tour layouts. Generic Floyd/Brent orbit finding is Miscellaneous `14-cyclefinding.hpp`; Mo on tree is Miscellaneous `10-offline_queries.hpp`.
- Matrix-tree, permanent, Pfaffian and polynomial kernels are Mathematics rows 23, 48, 14, 40; `53-graph_counting.hpp` only adapts graphs to them. Labeled graph-class counts by vertex count are Mathematics generating-function work.
- Manhattan MST and geometric graphs are Geometry `22-geometricmst.hpp`; radix and skew/persistent heaps are Data Structures 31 and 48.
- Bounded-flow ownership, numeric contracts, legacy migration obligations and archive leads are in [80-notes.md](80-notes.md).
