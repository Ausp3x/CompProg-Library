# Sweep Ledgers

Ledgers of library-wide sweeps. Each keeps the user's binding decisions under "Recorded Decisions". [duplicates.md](duplicates.md): wrappers and second implementations from `/duplicate-sweep`, read by `/prune`, `/package` and `@reviewer`. [omissions.md](omissions.md): completeness gaps from `/omission-sweep`, applied by `/omission-sweep apply`. [process.md](process.md): process ideas found between process batches, reviewed by `/reflect`. [Docs/00-omission-sweep.md](Docs/00-omission-sweep.md): evidence of the 2026-11-08 sweep (P254). [Docs/00-duplicate-rereview.md](Docs/00-duplicate-rereview.md): evidence of the 2026-11-10 duplicate and omission re-review (P275). [Docs/00-operation-renames.md](Docs/00-operation-renames.md): evidence of the 2026-11-10 operation-name renames (P276). [Docs/00-catalog-survey.md](Docs/00-catalog-survey.md): the 2026-11-27 survey behind the completeness-sweep catalogs, and the application of the candidates its samples found.
