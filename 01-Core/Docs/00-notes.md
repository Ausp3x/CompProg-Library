# 01 Core — notes

Folder-wide contracts, numeric policies and cross-folder ownership. Operation lists live only in [00-index.md](../00-index.md); sources in [00-sources.md](00-sources.md).

## Required groups

- In an `existing-unverified` row, the `required:` groups list operations the existing header does not yet provide; they stay planned until implementation and evidence establish otherwise.

## Ownership boundaries

- Integer roots (isqrt/icbrt/kth root with remainder, perfect-power tests) on native words are Mathematics `21-integer_roots.hpp`; overflow-checked powers and checked or saturating add and mul are Mathematics `01-mod_arithmetic.hpp`; InfInt keeps its own big-integer roots.
- Primitive roots, discrete logarithms, composite-modulus roots, CRT and finite-field extensions are Mathematics families; a residue type does not claim them.
- Markov-chain models and stationary-distribution theory are Mathematics `26-probability.hpp`; SparseMatrix provides only the iteration primitives of its row.
- Succinct and dynamic rank/select bit vectors are Data Structures `40`/`49`; `std::bitset` stays the compact fixed-size alternative.
- The incremental XOR linear basis and its prefix/range variants are Data Structures `62-xorbasis.hpp`; BitMatrix owns packed GF(2) matrices, elimination and subspace operations.

## Reduction and modular types

- Define exact modulus and dividend ranges, canonical versus lazy residues, wide intermediate bounds, zero/modulus-one handling, aliasing and transform boundaries. Barrett applies to every nonzero word modulus including even ones; Montgomery requires an odd modulus. Never infer full unsigned coverage merely from the storage width.
- Modular types state normalized negative/min-signed construction, safe raw construction, the meaning of ordering (canonical representatives), division by nonunits, negative exponents and batch inverse. Static primality knowledge and runtime primality assumptions differ (`prm` hint on `setMod`). Every modulus change invalidates values and caches under the documented rule; IDs and widths remain independent.
- Modulus one is the zero ring: zero is the identity and a unit; prime-root APIs are unavailable there.
- Left out of Barrett and Montgomery, with reasons: `div`, `multiplier` (wrappers: `divMod(x).first`, `Multiplier(r, b)`); a divisibility test (saves one multiply over `reduce(x) == 0`, needs a ceiling reciprocal and a narrower dividend; no contest catalog has it); signed-dividend reduction (the signed constructors of `05-modint.hpp` own normalization); a Shoup quotient (`NTL MulDivRem` is deprecated upstream); Barrett `add`/`sub`/`neg` (trivial, owned by the modular types); `pow` with a fixed factor (squaring gains nothing); lazy Barrett outputs (the correction is one compare-subtract on an independent product and no chained lazy domain exists for ordinary words); a 128-bit dividend for Barrett32 and array `reduce` (no catalog; fold the high word or use Barrett64); Lemire's 32-bit direct remainder (only 32-bit dividends, same two multiplies); Montgomery `neg`/`one`/lazy equality/`square` (`sub(0, a)`, `init(1)`, `normalize` comparison, no word-size squaring gain); residue inverses and batched powers (`05-modint.hpp` owns `inv`, the Montgomery power threshold and Miller–Rabin); 64-bit, AVX-512/IFMA and signed-Montgomery lanes (no 64x64 AVX2 multiply, ISA set is AVX2/FMA/BMI2, signed form is a Poly NTT kernel decision).
- Mini reductions, by design: no primality flags/detection, prime roots, batch inverse, decimal streams, integer casts, truthiness, ordering or increment/decrement; `val()` passes residues outward. `ModInt61` (Mersenne) is a full-type addition only. `!=` is rewritten from `==` in C++20 and mixed integer operands convert through the constructor.
- Left out of the modular types, with reasons: extra aliases such as `modint1000000007`/`mint107` (one alias; `ModInt<1000000007>` is one token longer); `umod()` (`mod()` already returns the unsigned word); a `randgen`/random hash base on `ModInt61` (Strings `randomBase` owns it); FPS constant-term hooks `log(mint)`/`exp(mint)` (Poly conventions, not residue arithmetic); `ntt_info`/`can_ntt`/primitive-root descriptors (Poly root cache and Mathematics own primitive roots); `std::hash`, `to_string`, `balanced()` signed representative (no catalog or no demonstrated need).
- Arithmetic modulo 2^64 is native `ulng` wraparound; a dedicated residue type was not adopted, and the odd-unit inverse is Mathematics `01-mod_arithmetic.hpp` `invMod2p64`.

## Reduction family

- `03-barrett.hpp` and `04-montgomery.hpp` (P004) specify machine-word arithmetic only: `05-modint.hpp` owns modular integer types, primality and residue inverses, and multiword truncated/folded Barrett, Montgomery–Svoboda, FastREDC and McLaughlin multiplication belong to `07-infint.hpp` and `16-poly.hpp` (MCA §§2.4.1–2.4.4 and §2.5, [00-sources.md](00-sources.md#previous-source-keys-and-audit-narrative)).
- Backend choice: Barrett for even moduli, independent products and arbitrary wide dividends (no representation conversion); Montgomery for an odd modulus with many chained products (convert once, convert back); `Barrett32::Multiplier` or `Barrett64::Multiplier` for a repeated factor. Modulus one returns zero throughout, including exponent zero.
- Special-prime, floating-point quotient, IFMA/AVX-512, GPU and residue-number-system variants need separate domain proofs and measured workloads; no floating-point exactness or constant-time behaviour is promised. Benchmarks: [03-barrett.md](03-barrett.md#benchmarks), [04-montgomery.md](04-montgomery.md#benchmarks).

## Integers

- Rational ([09-rational.md](09-rational.md)): values exclude NaN/infinity and never use InfInt's sentinel; the header does not include `07-infint.hpp` (Codeforces' 64 KB source limit), so `Rational<InfInt>` callers include it. Not adopted (2026-10-17 sweep): `height`, a rational `gcd`, `%`/`divMod`, `mulPow2`, integer-argument overloads and accessor aliases; approximation, Stern–Brocot and continued fractions are Mathematics `20-continued_fraction.hpp`.
- InfInt sentinel: the full type keeps the signed infinity flag, the `inf` token and the saturating native casts; the exact rules per operation and the finite-only assertions are in [07-infint.md](07-infint.md), Representation and sentinel. The mini follows the same rules on its shared operations.
- Not adopted for InfInt (2026-10-17 sweep, [00-sources.md](00-sources.md)): `divRound`/`remSymmetric` (FLINT-only rounding modes, derivable from `divModFloor`), `ilog`/`sizeInBase` (`toString(base).size()` and `bitLength`), `toDouble2Exp`/`toLongDouble` (`toDouble` after a shift by `bitLength`), `PreparedDivisor` as a public type (cached reciprocals stay internal to `powMod` and radix conversion), `multiModReduce`/`crtBig` product and remainder trees (Mathematics CRT rows), `sampleRuns`/`RedundantBinary` (test generator and a separate structure), Toom-4/6.5/8.5 and Schönhage–Strassen (the measured transforms cover the sizes above Toom-3; Fürer and Harvey–van der Hoeven, never swept, have no measured crossover either), small-quotient and block-Barrett division (covered by Knuth D for short quotients and the cached-reciprocal block division), exact remainder, subquadratic Jacobi and half-GCD-based extended GCD as separate names (the half-GCD matrices already drive `exGcd`), `UInt` carry-out forms, `sqr`, `gcd` and `isqrtRem` (convert to `InfInt`). `nextPrime`/`prevPrime`/`primorial` have no owner: Mathematics rows 05 and 36 are the candidates (handoff in [07-infint.md](07-infint.md)).
- Mini InfInt: identical results to InfInt on the shared domain ([08-infintmini.md](08-infintmini.md)). Left out: `++`/`--`, `lcm`, floating and 128-bit conversions out, limb shifts, roots, bitwise operations, modular inverses and every other full-type extra.

## Matrices

- State operation requirements individually: semiring products need no division; field elimination does; composite residue rings require unit pivots or ring-safe methods (Bareiss, CRT, Berkowitz). Finite-field rank is not an epsilon test.
- Full Matrix must derive moduli through `T::mod()` with correct word widths and replace static field constraints with a runtime contract for dynamic types; see the [C03 handoff](05-modint.md#dependent-owner-handoff). Reconstruct matrices and cached factors after dynamic modulus changes.
- Approximate algebra is separately named and API-scoped: pivoting/rank tolerance policy, conditioning, residual/error criteria and nonconvergence are stated; Cholesky requires positive-definite symmetric/Hermitian input and pivoted LDL its stated indefinite domain. Complex conjugation is distinct from transpose. Add numerical extensions only with defensible stability evidence.
- Structured solves (Toeplitz/Hankel/Vandermonde, banded/tridiagonal including cyclic, rank-one updates) state unit-pivot conditions and a generic fallback. The existing `hafnian` is preserved and audited; permanent/Pfaffian/Hafnian/loop-Hafnian carry explicit characteristic assumptions, and Mathematics `48-combinatorial_linear_algebra.hpp` owns the scalar research APIs (duplication allowed).
- GF(2): packed layouts, padding bits, rectangular multiplication, Four Russians crossover, row/column spaces and intersection, all-solution witnesses and word-boundary cases. Boolean OR-AND reachability multiplication is a separately named semiring operation, never GF(2) multiplication.
- Sparse: sorted indices, duplicates and explicit zeros, rectangular/empty shapes, transpose/conversions, dense crossover and fill-in. Wiedemann/block Wiedemann/Lanczos and random preconditioning need field-size assumptions and Monte Carlo versus Las Vegas guarantees. `steadyState` performs a fixed number of products and tests no convergence; keep it under an honest contract and specify `stationaryState` separately with residual, convergence and periodic/reducible-chain behaviour. CG requires SPD/Hermitian PD; GMRES/BiCGSTAB and preconditioners need separate convergence/breakdown contracts.
- Mini matrices: `MatrixMini` over stated fields only; `BitMatrixMini` provides elimination, rank, consistency, one solution and a nullspace basis; `SparseMatrixMini` keeps dimensions, triplet ingestion with duplicate/zero rules, vector product and repeated-product powers.

## Polynomials and FPS

- Representation: coefficient order, zero normalization versus retained FPS precision, truncation, ring/field/characteristic constraints and sparse/dense crossover. Integers/rationals, prime fields, composite residues and approximate coefficients have distinct supported operation sets; characteristic-induced nonexistence or nonuniqueness must be visible (exp/log/sqrt/kth root conditions, valuation and branch choices).
- Convolution: FFT with a quantified exactness envelope; NTT with a documented root cache lifetime and maximum transform length; arbitrary-mod CRT with signed reconstruction. Convolution modulo 2^64 and over binary extension fields are separate exact domains, also duplicated as scalar Mathematics `65-convolution_specialized.hpp`.
- Evaluation/interpolation: repeated points require Hermite data or explicit rejection. Composition names its algorithm (Brent–Kung baseline; Kinoshita–Li as the researched faster alternative) and reversion its method (Lagrange inversion via power projection).
- General multivariate systems, Gröbner bases, polynomial matrices, set power series and specialized combinatorial counts remain Mathematics families, not promises of the univariate type.
- `17-polymini.hpp`: its NTT uses the same roots and bit-reversed order as `Poly<T>::ntt`, so every shared operation returns the same coefficients and sizes ([17-polymini.md](17-polymini.md)). The row names Berlekamp–Massey `findLinearRecurrence`, the full type's name, so code moves between the two unchanged. Left out after the 2026-10-10 sweep, with reasons: `sqrt` (for `a[0] = 1` it is `exp(log(a, n) * (T(1) / 2), n)`; the general constant term needs a modular square root the mini does not carry), `powMod` and `x^k mod f` (`linearRecurrenceKth` covers the contest use), chirp-z, `gcd`/`resultant`, composition and its inverse, sample-point shifts, sparse series, shifts and truncation helpers (vector operations), `T * a` with the scalar first, unary minus and compound assignment (`a * c`, `a - b` and `a = a * b` say the same), arbitrary-mod convolution and Lagrange point evaluation (full `Poly` and Mathematics own them).
- `16-poly.hpp` API shape (type-header rule): algorithms on `Poly` values are friends found through ADL (`exp(f, n)`, `gcd(a, b)`), builders and vector transforms are static members (`Poly<T>::interpolate`, `Poly<T>::ntt`), and the type-independent integer, GF(2^k) and double-FFT entry points are `PolyCompanion::` statics; no public free function exists, so contest code and Mathematics headers call these forms.
- `16-poly.hpp` left out, with reasons: `remove`/`inflate`/`deflate` (index loops, no algorithm); `compose_exp` and the Möbius or `e^x - 1` special compositions (same `O(n log^2 n)` bound through `compose`); bivariate rational coefficient extraction (`inv2d` covers the bivariate inverse; no contest catalog beyond maspypy); modular `MinPolyMod`/`TraceMod`/`ProjectPowers` (Mathematics `42-finite_fields.hpp` owns extension-field minimal polynomials; the transposed modular composition has no contest use); `power_sums_to_poly` (`exp` of the log form); orthogonal, Fibonacci and q-series polynomials (FLINT only); online FPS inverse/exp/log/pow (online `sqrt` has no owner), composed sum and product, subset-sum products, Schönhage–Strassen, Goldilocks-specific NTT (Mathematics `52`, `46`, `12`, `65` own them, and the generic `Ntt<T>` already handles any 64-bit NTT prime through the scalar path).

## Template and debug

- The template stays a preamble: exact-width aliases, the three legacy macros, PBDS `indexed_set`/`indexed_map`, finite sentinels and `chmin`/`chmax`. Not adopted from the 2026-10-06 catalog sweep: `sz`/`sze` (explicit `int(v.size())` is the library style and the legacy macro was already retired), `all`/`ral` (collide with `std::views::all`; explicit begin/end), `rep`/`FOR` loops, `pii`/`vi`/`ll` aliases (duplicate the exact-width aliases), `eb`, `pq_min`, `infty<T>`, `#pragma` lines and I/O setup (forbidden in headers), seeded `rng` (`06-Miscellaneous/01-random.hpp`), custom-hash `gp_hash_table` (`06-Miscellaneous/02-customhash.hpp`), `indexed_multiset` (`02-Data Structures/07-ordered_set.hpp`), bit helpers (`06-Miscellaneous/06-bit_operations.hpp`), `floorDiv`/`ceilDiv` (`05-Mathematics/01-mod_arithmetic.hpp`), `y_combinator` (`06-Miscellaneous/24-memoization.hpp`), fast I/O and timers (`06-Miscellaneous`).
- Debug prints values on one line per `debug` call. Not adopted: one-row-per-line matrices (slice rows and call `debug` per row), `INF` spelled as `inf`, pointer/pointee traversal, `dbg::hex`/type names/value-returning `dbg`, colour variants and colour auto-off, `dbg_only`, file/function names (contest code is one file), ASCII tree drawing (graph tooling), `std::function` (not printable); elapsed-time helpers belong to `06-Miscellaneous/49-timer.hpp`.

## Bitset

- Shared primitive for BitMatrix, subset DP and graph search. Define size mismatch, padding, shifts at/above length, tail masking, proxy invalidation and mutation aliasing. The generic bitset must not depend on BitMatrix (no dependency cycle).
- Bitset, not adopted from the 2026-10-06 sweep: `join`/append (resize plus `combineRange`), `reversed`, index collection and `forEach` visitors (`findNext` loops), intersection visitors (`countAnd`/`&`), prefix parity, checked `toUllong` (`blocks()[0]`), `isProperSubsetOf`, `lowbit` (`findFirst`), lexicographic ordering and hashing (documented exclusions in [18-bitset.md](18-bitset.md)).
