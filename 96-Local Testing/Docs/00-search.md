## Contracts

### Documents

- One document per inventory row of `01-Core` … `08-Python` (`0[1-8]-*/00-index.md`, rows whose first cell is a `.hpp` or `.py` code span). Planned rows, `08-Python` modules and the template and debug headers are included (user decision 2026-11-07 for the last); `show` marks a module with `Python` and a header the contest template already holds (grab's `EXCLUDED`) with `in template` after its status.
- Tier: the current `## Basic|Advanced|Esoteric` section; a `01-Core` row, which has no tier section, is `Basic` when its Basic cell starts with the word `yes` (`yes until mini exists`, and `yes when implemented` for a planned mini), else `Advanced` (user decision 2026-11-07). Status: the first of `verified`, `partial`, `existing-unverified`, `legacy-reference`, `planned` the Status cell starts with.
- Fields: `stem` (the file stem without `NN-`/`_NN_` (`bare`), split at `_`; a word of it whose stem is no known word but which splits into two or more pieces that are known words or stop words by their stems, each of `PART` (2) letters or more and at most one shorter than 3, also yields the stems of its pieces other than stop words: the fewest pieces, on a tie without a short piece, then with the longest pieces from the end (`twosat` → `two sat`, not `twos at`; `disjointsparsetable` → `disjoint sparse table`, not `disjoints parse table`); `segmenttree` → `segment tree`, `mergesorttree` → `merge sort tree`, `meetinthemiddle` → `meet middle`, while `rindex` stays whole, not `ri nd ex`; a known word is a token of `PART` letters or more in any row's `ops`, `qual` or `aliases`), `ident` (the stem without `_` and every grab name's `::` parts whole except `operator…` parts, lower case, split at spaces only: `dialdijkstra`), `ops` (the Operations cell without qualifiers), `qual` (the parenthesized qualifiers), `aliases` (below), `folder` (the folder name without its prefix). Every field's text passes `words` and `stem` below, except `ident`, which is only stemmed (`dialdijkstra` stays one token, `segmenttrees` would lose its `s`).
- Aliases: from the row's evidence document only (`<folder>/Docs/<NN-name>.md`; for a module, when `08-Python` gains them, `08-Python/Docs/<module stem>.md`, as `_03_number_theory.md`) — every line of a section whose heading starts with `Aliases`, and every paragraph or list opened by a line `<optional word> Aliases …:` with no backtick before the colon (`Aliases:`, `Catalog aliases:`, `- Aliases (2026-10-19 sweep):`, `- **Aliases elsewhere:**`, `Search aliases (P263, grab --find):`), until a blank line or heading; the opening label up to its colon (and closing `**`) is dropped from each line, so `Search`, `P263` or `grab` index nothing; URLs and link targets dropped. `Aliases with \`FIXED\`:` opens nothing. What a package lists there (`/package` step 3): the row's other English names and abbreviations, its OI Wiki page title, and the Chinese names contestants use, in Simplified and mainland usage; a Taiwan or Hong Kong term is listed too only when `grab --find` with it does not reach the row through the conversion below, written in Simplified as its regional reading (`普林算法` for `普林演算法`, `连通组件` for `連通元件`), so that the documents hold no phrase a query's regional reading replaces, apart from the mainland words `test_repo_chinese` pins (Chinese, below). Items: the aliases text, folded and without complexity expressions (`words` below), cut at `,` `;` `:` `(` `)` `|` (their fullwidth forms fold to them), `、` and line ends, never inside a code span (`` `flow(s, t, MAX, 0)` `` is one piece, not the items `t` and `0`); a piece cut at a colon labels what follows and is no item (`OI Wiki` in `(OI Wiki: 线段树)`). An item whose tokens equal the query's ranks its row in the alias class (Ranking), so an item names its row whole, once: no qualifier of its own in parentheses (`BFS（搜索）` put the traversal row first for `搜索`, "search").
- Names (what a hit offers to grab), in cell order: a type head (capitalized, `::`-qualified or marked `(alias`, as `iint (alias)`, which a label `aliases:` is not; a head of several words gives its capitalized ones, `A as B and C`) and `Type::member` for its entries (`ctor` names the type; an entry the cell qualifies itself stays as written, `PolyCompanion::fft`, and a `std::` entry is a specialization, no name); a camelCase function head alone (`cartesianTree (cmp): parent, root` → `cartesianTree`); the entries of a lower-case label head (`required:`) or of no head. Template arguments are stripped; operators stay `Type::operator+=`.

### Tokens

- `words`: NFKC first (fullwidth letters and digits, compatibility ideographs), then a Latin letter with diacritics becomes the letter its canonical decomposition begins with (`latin`: `Prüfer` → `prufer`, `Thuật` → `thuat`, `Ǣ` → `æ`; `ø` and `ß`, which have none, stay, and so do Greek and Cyrillic letters) (`fold`); then a complexity expression is dropped (`unbounded`: `O`, `o`, `Θ` or `Ω` after no letter or digit, then a parenthesized stretch with its nested parentheses, as `O(N+M)` and `O(T(n) log n)`; an unclosed one stays), since it names no concept; lower case; identifiers split at `_`, `-`, camelCase, acronym runs (`LCPArray` → `lcp array`) and letter-digit boundaries; a run of Chinese characters is cut from letters and digits and gives character pairs (Chinese, below); English stop words (`a an and as at by for from in into is it of on or the to via with`) dropped, Chinese never.
- `stem`: a plural first: `ies` → `y` and `ches/shes/sses/xes/zzes` lose `es` (words over 4 letters), a final `s` goes unless after `s`, `u` or `i` (words over 3 letters); then `-ing` and a final `e` as the Snowball Porter2 stemmer's steps 1b and 5 treat them: `-ing` goes when the rest holds a vowel, then an `at`, `bl` or `iz` ending or a short word gets its `e` back and a doubled `bb dd gg mm nn pp rr tt` ending a stem of 4 letters or more loses one letter; a final `e` goes in R2, or in R1 when not after a short syllable. So a word and its `-ing` form share a stem (`sliding`, `slide` → `slide`; `merging`, `merge` → `merg`; `spanning` → `span`; `adding` → `add`; `string` stays). Irregular: `vertices`, `indices`, `matrices`, `series`, `species`, `tries`, `alias`/`aliases`, and `unite`, `unites`, `uniting`, kept apart from `unit`. Among the 3,150 ASCII alphabetic words `words` gives for the header stems, Operations cells, folder names and alias texts, a word and its `s`, `es`, `ies` or `-ing` form share a stem unless they are different words (`prim`/`primes`, `plan`/`planes`, `not`/`notes`, `on`/`ones`, `fac`/`faces`, `str`/`string`) or the form has 3 letters or fewer, which keeps its `s` (`ops`, `ids`, `its`) so that `lis` and `sos` stay whole.
- Typo tolerance per query token absent from the vocabulary: optimal string alignment distance (adjacent swaps count 1) at most 0 up to 3 letters, 1 up to `TOLERANCE[1]` (7), 2 beyond (`TOLERANCE`); a match at distance 1 counts 0.85, at 2 0.8 (`TYPO`). A token of `PREFIX_MIN` (2) letters or more also matches the tokens it begins, at `PREFIX` (1.0). Each query token takes its best alternative per row.
- Joins: every 2 and 3 adjacent query words, none Chinese, joined (`block cut tree` → `blockcuttree`) are extra query tokens, matched as typed or within typo tolerance, never as a prefix.

### Chinese

- A run of Chinese characters (CJK Unified Ideographs with Extension A, compatibility ideographs after NFKC, planes 2 and 3) gives the overlapping character pairs (a lone character itself) of its one form, and in a query then the new pairs of its regional reading. Pairs are tokens like words: weighted, matched exactly (2 letters are below every typo tolerance), never joined (they overlap already), and adjacent pairs earn the phrase bonus. A lone query character stands for every token holding it, a pair at the prefix factor and itself at 1, each scaled to the character's own idf over the rows holding it in any token, so a common character weighs what its frequency says: `AVL 树` lists `AVLTree` first, `樹` still finds `树状数组` and `线段树`.
- One form (`simplified`): a run is Traditional when it holds a character outside GB2312 (the mainland character set, by Python's `gb2312` codec) that the character tables convert. A Traditional run goes through OpenCC's conversion as OpenCC's `tw2s` and `hk2s` chain it: cut at the longest Traditional-to-Simplified phrase keys (each key one segment, each stretch between keys one), then each segment through the Taiwan and Hong Kong variant phrases and variant characters, then through the Traditional-to-Simplified phrases and characters; within one such group, at each position the longest key of the first dictionary holding a key there is replaced, else the character stays. Then every run has its characters converted by the character table (a character both scripts write, as `乾`, takes one form) and its words replaced by their mainland class's word where `mw` lists one (`想像` and `想象` as one; a longer phrase `mw` lists is a class of its own, so a variant spelling inside it can stay apart: `想像出来` beside `想象出来`). A run without such a character is mainland text, kept as written apart from those two steps, so `查询` stays `查询`.
- Regional reading (`simplified(text, regional)`): any run, Traditional or not, read as Taiwan or Hong Kong text: the same chain with the regional phrases first in the variant group, as OpenCC's `tw2sp` chains them, so Taiwan's and Hong Kong's words for a thing the mainland names otherwise become the mainland's (`演算法`, `陣列`, `遞迴`, `字尾` → `算法`, `数组`, `递归`, `后缀`). A query's run adds the new pairs of that reading, never substituting them: a query in Taiwan words, also in characters both scripts share (`字尾`, `函式`, `字元`), reaches the rows the mainland words name, and keeps its own pairs. A query reads with the regional phrases less those a row's text holds as written (inside one run of Chinese characters of any field, in its one form), since mainland text uses them as mainland words: `序列`, `外接`, `文件`, `行程` and `宏`, which `test_repo_chinese` pins, so `子序列` gains no `子串行` (with `子串`, "substring") and `外接圆` no `外置圆`; a phrase only Traditional writes is in no one form and keeps its reading (`查詢` adds `查找` to `查询`). Documents keep their own pairs only (aliases are mainland text, Contracts above), so a word counts once. Of the 9,448 mainland runs below, 69 gain any pair as a query, mostly synonyms (`建立`/`创建`, `新增`/`添加`).
- Table (`_11_search_t2s.tsv`, 6,840 entries, `name<TAB>key<TAB>value` after one header line): six dictionaries from OpenCC's `data/dictionary` at commit `fae2a0a`, keys and values all Chinese characters (the search converts runs of them only): `ts` (498) the first values of `TSPhrases`; `tc` (4,116) those of `TSCharacters`, under those of the `TSCharactersExt` OpenCC builds from its `@tofu-risk` lines (the first value other than the key), changes only; `vp` (1,166) `HKVariantsRevPhrases` under `TWVariantsRevPhrases`; `vc` (80) the reverse of `HKVariants` under `TWVariants`, as OpenCC's `reverse.py` builds it (an `@reverse-prefer` key first), changes only; `rp` (805) `HKPhrasesRev` under `TWPhrasesRev`; `mw` (175) each word of the classes an `STPhrases` or `TSPhrases` pair links (the mainland side through `tc`, the other through `ts` and `tc`, then `tc`) to its class's word (most often a mainland side, then the shortest, in the basic CJK block, the lowest) where that changes it, plus the identities a class's word needs to stay whole (`富于想像` is no `富于` + `想象`). The recipe is under Sources.
- Fidelity, measured against the OpenCC 1.4.2 Python package on the 9,451 distinct runs of Chinese characters in the OI Wiki nav, 20 OI Wiki pages and every Markdown file of `01`–`08` (74,890 characters, 9,448 runs read as mainland): the one form changes no mainland run and is idempotent on all 9,451. Each mainland run converted by OpenCC's `s2t`, `s2tw` and `s2hk` (8,309, 8,303 and 8,308 results read as Traditional): the one form of every result read as Traditional equals that of OpenCC's own `t2s`, `tw2s` and `hk2s` output, and every result's pairs as a query hold all the run's pairs as a document (the 7 `s2tw` results in characters GB2312 holds, as `么半群` and `意味著`, through their regional pairs). With `s2twp` (Taiwan words too, 8,266 read as Traditional), 125 results miss some of the run's pairs: OpenCC's own `tw2sp` loses them as well for 115 (`默认` written `預設` comes back `预设`), and the other 10 hold `執行`, which the pinned data's `TWPhrasesRev` keeps where OpenCC 1.4.2 gives `運行`. The regional reading with the whole table differs from OpenCC 1.4.2's `tw2sp` on 32 `s2tw` and 39 `s2twp` results, all at `執行`, `程序` or `行程`, the entries whose first value the pinned data changed. Over OpenCC's own 49,409 mainland phrases (the Simplified sides of `STPhrases` and `TSPhrases` in GB2312 characters), the one form of their `s2t`, `s2hk` and `s2tw` results differs from theirs, and their pairs as a query miss some of the phrase's, on 2, 2 and 4, each through a variant character (`么凤士多`, `六须鲶`; for `s2tw` also `凑合着`, `显着标志`).

### Ranking

- Score: BM25F, `idf = ln(1 + (N − df + 0.5)/(df + 0.5))`, pseudo frequency `Σ_f w_f · tf_f / (1 − b + b · len_f/avg_f)`, saturation `x(k1 + 1)/(k1 + x)`, summed over query tokens (each at its best alternative's factor); plus, for each two query tokens adjacent in the query's own reading or in its regional reading (`後綴陣列` reads `后缀 缀阵 阵列` and `后缀 缀数 数组`, so it earns the bonus `后缀数组` earns, and no pair across the two), the phrase bonus of their best alternatives adjacent in some field of the row, `phrase · (f_a · idf_a + f_b · idf_b)/2` with each alternative's factor `f` (a typo, a prefix or a lone character's token counts at its factor, so a lone character's bonus rests on its own idf); times the row's status factor and tier factor (`STANDING`, `LEVEL`), so finished and simpler rows rise (user request 2026-11-07).
- Order: (1) rows whose stem tokens equal the query's (`montgomery` lists `04-montgomery.hpp` before `ModInt`'s `Context::montgomery`); (2) rows with a name equal to the query (case, fullwidth forms, Latin diacritics and complexity expressions ignored, also its last `::` part); (3) rows with an alias item whose tokens equal the query's or those of its regional reading (`线段树` and `線段樹` list `03-segmenttree.hpp`, `并查集` `01-dsu.hpp`, `bit` `02-fenwick.hpp` before `BitOps`); (4) for a one-word query, rows with a name holding it as a run of whole words (`bit` in `BitOps`, not `sam` in `same`) or as pieces of 3 letters or more beginning consecutive words of the name (`segtree` in `SegmentTree` and `SegTreeBeats`, `lazyseg` in `LazySegmentTree`); words with spaces describe a concept, so `binary search` lifts no `parallelBinarySearch`; (5) the rest with a positive score. Only a C++ row that is `verified`, `partial` or `existing-unverified` takes classes 1 to 4: a module, a planned or a legacy-reference row has nothing grab copies, so it ranks by score alone. Classes 1 to 4 order by tier (Basic, Advanced, Esoteric), status (order above), score, header path: simplest and most finished first, so `bipartite` lists `bipartiteCheck` before `BipartiteMatching` and `segtree` lists `SegmentTree` first. Class 5 orders by score, tier, status, header path. `-n K` (default 10) keeps the first K; nothing positive prints `no match`.
- Name shown: the equal name, else the shortest whole-word or shortened match, else the name sharing the most query idf, a name's words including its entry's parenthesized qualifiers (`miller rabin` shows `isPrime`, whose entry reads `isPrime (deterministic Miller-Rabin)`), then the most words of the header stem, the stem joined into one word counting too (`segtreeBuild` for `01-seg_tree.hpp`), the first on a tie; the header path when no name shares a query word and the row is no own-stem match, so an alias-only match names no unrelated function (`kadane` shows `06-Miscellaneous/08-sequence_algorithms.hpp`, `字尾` shows `07-Strings/12-sais.hpp`), or when a row has no names (user decision 2026-11-07).
- Output line: name (`-` when the name shown is the header, which grab cannot take), header, tier and status, each column as wide as its longest entry in the list with no trailing spaces, then `  Python` for a module and `  in template` for a template or debug header. An unknown name's error (libclang path and whole-header fallback) appends `closest rows (name, header, tier, status):` and up to 3 such lines, indented.
- Cost and freshness: every run parses the inventories and evidence documents anew (466 rows, about 0.5 s with the Chinese table) and needs no libclang, cache or index, so a status change, a new row or a new alias line ranks from the next run on; a query takes about 9 ms (median 8 ms, 95th percentile 18 ms, at most 45 ms over the 1,706 queries of the list), and `grab --find shortest path` about 0.55 s in all (2026-11-08, three runs each, the machine otherwise idle). `test_repo_corpus` reads the live inventories, so a renamed or removed corpus target, or a ranking drop below a floor, fails the tester.

### Weights and cutoffs

The query list is the tester's full mode with `--seed 1` (`corpus` in the tester): the corpus file's queries in file order, then the Library Checker problems in ledger order, then, source `generated`, `typo_variant(query, random.Random(1))` of each `lc` and `cp` query in that order (one random swap, deletion, substitution or insertion inside its longest word of 5+ letters, the first letter kept). The objective is the mean over the ten sources `cp oi abbr typo simple py lc generated zhs zht` of (recall@5 + MRR@10)/2; `zh` stays outside, since it checks the OI Wiki titles P263 copied into the aliases. "Even" and "odd" are the queries at even and odd 0-based positions of the list.

- Search (`14-search_tester.py --tune all|even [--start JSON] [--jobs N]`): coordinate descent from the module's constants, or `--start` over them, on the list (`all`) or its even half (`even`). Each parameter in turn, in the order below, moves to the best value of its grid with the current value added (the current one kept on a tie); a best value at the grid's edge grows the grid by the parameter's step (`stem`, `ident` 0.5; `ops` 0.25; `aliases` 1; `folder` 0.3; `k1` 0.8; `phrase` 0.5; `tol2`, `prefix_min`, `part` 1; the rest 0.1) while the grown set stays valid. Valid sets (`tune_valid`): every factor in (0, 1], with 0 < `t2` ≤ `t1`, planned ≤ existing-unverified and planned < 1 (the user's request that planned rows rank below implemented ones), Esoteric ≤ Advanced; `b` in (0, 1]; `qual`, `phrase` and `folder` at least 0, the other weights and `k1` positive; `tol2` above the first length; `prefix_min` and `part` at least 1. Rounds repeat until one moves nothing (at most 10). Grids: `stem`, `ident` 0.5 1 1.5 2 3; `ops` 0.5 0.75 1 1.5 2; `qual` 0 0.1 0.25 0.5 1; `aliases` 1 1.5 2 2.5 3 4; `folder` 0 0.3 0.6 0.9 1.2; `b` 0.2 0.3 0.5 0.75 0.9; `k1` 0.6 0.9 1.2 1.6 2.4; `phrase` 0 0.5 1 1.5 2; `prefix` 0.45 0.6 0.75 0.9; `t1` 0.5 0.6 0.7 0.85; `t2` 0.3 … 0.7; planned (legacy-reference alike) 0.6 … 0.9; existing-unverified and Advanced 0.8 0.9 1; Esoteric 0.6 … 1; `tol2` 6 … 9; `prefix_min` 2 3 4; `part` 2 3. Verified, partial and Basic stay 1. It prints every step, the values and the module constants, then with `all` the per-source results and the floors they give, with `even` the odd half's objective of the start, the tuned values and the module's constants.
- The first `TOLERANCE` length stays 3 (second run): the list misspells only words of 5+ letters, and on 302 extra queries misspelled in a 4-letter word (each corpus and Library Checker query holding one, in list order, with `random.Random(2)`: a random such word, then a random swap at position 1 or 2 of differing letters, substitution at 1 to 3 or insertion at 1 to 4) a first length of 3 scored 0.917 / 0.792 against 0.864 / 0.755 for 4.

- Every weight, factor and cutoff is the descent's choice (user decision 2026-11-07: "whichever is best"), the first tolerance length excepted for the reason above. Kept as set, since the corpus cannot measure them (user decision 2026-11-07, which chose tuning for the cutoffs only): grab's 3 concept rows below an unknown name (`CONCEPTS`), floors 0.02 below the results, the corpus mapped by subagents from the inventories rather than by hand, and OI Wiki page slugs in place of article titles.

- Chinese (user decisions 2026-11-07, third run: "Full Chinese support", then "include traditional chinese"): Simplified and Traditional, Taiwan and Hong Kong usage.     lines = (src / name).read_text(encoding='utf-8').splitlines()
```python
    return ([(l.split('\t')[0], l.split('\t')[1].split(), lines[i - 1] if i else '') for i, l in enumerate(lines)
             if l.strip() and not l.startswith('#')], [l for l in lines if l.startswith('# @reverse-prefer:')])

def first(name, tofu=False):  # key -> first value; tofu: TSCharactersExt (an @tofu-risk entry's first value other than its key)
    d = {}
    for k, vs, before in entries(name)[0]:
        vs = ([v for v in vs if v != k] if before.startswith('# @tofu-risk:') else []) if tofu else vs
        if vs:
            d.setdefault(k, vs[0])
    return d

def reverse(name):  # data/scripts/reverse.py: value -> its first key, an @reverse-prefer key first
    es, prefs = entries(name)
    d = {}
    for k, vs, _ in es:
        for v in vs:
            d.setdefault(v, []).append(k)
    for line in prefs:
        f = line[len('# @reverse-prefer:'):].split()
        d[f[0]].insert(0, d[f[0]].pop(d[f[0]].index(f[-1])))
    return {k: v[0] for k, v in d.items()}

changes = lambda d: {k: v for k, v in d.items() if k != v}  # a character dictionary's identities change nothing
t = {'ts': first('TSPhrases.txt'),  # OpenCC's t2s, tw2s, tw2sp and hk2s dictionaries, Taiwan's over Hong Kong's
     'tc': changes({**first('TSCharacters.txt'), **first('TSCharacters.txt', tofu=True)}),  # TSCharactersExt first
     'vp': {**first('HKVariantsRevPhrases.txt'), **first('TWVariantsRevPhrases.txt')},
     'vc': changes({**reverse('HKVariants.txt'), **reverse('TWVariants.txt')}),
     'rp': {**first('HKPhrasesRev.txt'), **first('TWPhrasesRev.txt')}}
t = {n: {k: v for k, v in d.items() if HAN.fullmatch(k) and HAN.fullmatch(v)} for n, d in t.items()}  # the search converts
group = lambda *names: [(t[n], max(map(len, t[n]))) for n in names]  # runs of Chinese characters only (no PN結, no ·)
mainland = lambda text: replace(text, group('tc'))  # a mainland run's characters
standard = lambda text: mainland(replace(text, group('ts', 'tc')))  # a standard Traditional word as the search converts it

# mw: classes of words linked by a pair of STPhrases or TSPhrases, both sides as the search converts them (想像 and 想象);
# each member -> the class's word (the mainland side most often, then the shortest, in the basic block, the lowest),
# kept where it changes something, plus the identities a class's word needs to stay whole (富于想像 is no 富于 + 想象)
votes, parent = {}, {}
def find(x):
    while parent.setdefault(x, x) != x:
        x = parent[x]
    return x
for name, side in (('STPhrases.txt', 0), ('TSPhrases.txt', 1)):
    for k, vs, _ in entries(name)[0]:
        for v in vs:
            m, o = (mainland(v), standard(k)) if side else (mainland(k), standard(v))
            if HAN.fullmatch(m) and HAN.fullmatch(o):  # the search converts runs of Chinese characters only
                votes[m] = votes.get(m, 0) + 1
                parent[find(m)] = find(o)
classes = {}
for x in list(parent):
    classes.setdefault(find(x), []).append(x)
word = {x: min(ms, key=lambda m: (-votes.get(m, 0), len(m), not BASIC.fullmatch(m), m)) for ms in classes.values() for x in ms}
t['mw'] = changes(word)
while bad := {v for v in set(word.values()) if replace(v, group('mw')) != v}:
    t['mw'].update((v, v) for v in bad)
with out.open('w', encoding='utf-8', newline='\n') as f:
    f.write('# Third-party: derived from the Open Chinese Convert (OpenCC) dictionaries, https://github.com/ByVoid/OpenCC data/dictionary at '
            'fae2a0a911c30764e669a7d8b0591cd8e852934a, Apache-2.0 (see THIRD-PARTY.md): first values of TSPhrases (ts), TSCharacters '
            'under TSCharactersExt (tc), the Hong Kong under the Taiwan variant phrases (vp), variant characters (vc) and regional '
            'phrases (rp), and mainland word classes (mw), for _11_search.py by the recipe in 96-Local Testing/Docs/00-search.md\n')
    for kind, d in t.items():
        for k in sorted(d):
            f.write(f'{kind}\t{k}\t{d[k]}\n')
```
