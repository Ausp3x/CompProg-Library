# P012 — MA01 and MA02

## Scope and completion order

P012 owns six existing mathematics headers. MA01 was implemented and passed its full per-header verification before MA02 implementation began. Prerequisites P002/C01 and P005/C03 were already checked and their template/modular APIs were inspected. Other Mathematics inventory families and their separate packages remain outside this work.

| Batch | Header | Contract, source accounting and feature-to-test evidence |
|---|---|---|
| MA01 | `01-mod_arithmetic.hpp` | [Arithmetic](90-arithmetic.md): signed boundaries, wide gcd/lcm/division, checked and saturating add/multiply, checked integer powers, native modular arithmetic and the inverse mod 2^64. |
| MA01 | `02-search_algorithms.hpp` | [Search](91-search.md): integer and real binary/unimodal searches, Fibonacci-section and galloping searches, precision and termination status. |
| MA01 | `03-equation_solvers.hpp` | [Equations](92-equations.md): complete linear parameterizations, bounded boxes and congruences. |
| MA02 | `04-sieve_algorithms.hpp` | [Sieves](93-sieves.md): Eratosthenes, linear SPF/LPF, factors and multiplicative tables. |
| MA02 | `05-combinatorics.hpp` | [Combinatorics](94-combinatorics.md): prime factorial tables with large-n binomials, any-ring Pascal and derangement tables, checked exact counts, Fibonacci/Lucas, Catalan, ballot and derangements. |
| MA02 | `06-segmentedsieve.hpp` | [Segmented sieve](95-segmentedsieve.md): reusable blocked bases, plain/odd/wheel-30 streaming, factorization and interval migration. |

- **Completed operations.** `checkedAdd`, `checkedMul`, `saturatingAdd`, `saturatingMul` and `invMod2p64` in `01-mod_arithmetic.hpp`. The research sweep added `fibSearch` and `expSearch` to `02-search_algorithms.hpp`, and `combiLarge`, `binomialTable` and `derangementTable` to `05-combinatorics.hpp`. Sources are in [81-sources.md](81-sources.md); rejected candidates are in [80-notes.md](80-notes.md).
- **Rules.** Every header now meets the closing-brace rule and the comment cap. The in-code contracts moved to the `## Contracts` sections of [90](90-arithmetic.md)–[95](95-segmentedsieve.md). The code uses `int8_t` for Möbius tables and `lng` instead of `size_t` for indices.

The `@reviewer` pass found no correctness defect in the code. It confirmed one evidence error: the ulng-offset bound in 95 ignored the Wheel30 walk, and the bound is now corrected. Its lower-confidence notes were also applied: a `-Wconversion` chained assignment in `getTables`, the shadowing constructor parameter in `PrimeCombinatorics`, the derangement recurrence that duplicated `derangementTable`, and table tests for `n = 0, 1`. The reviewer independently probed `fibSearch` (151,280 shapes), every odd `a < 2 * 10^6` for `invMod2p64`, and the segmented sieve at `[INT64_MAX - 3000, INT64_MAX)` against Miller–Rabin.

The oracles are Python unbounded integers and built-in modular powers and inverses, matrix powers, enumerated arrangements and paths, trial factors and divisors, divisor-inversion identities, analytic monotone and minimum positions, linear scans, and direct enumeration of bounded equations. Finite tests are paired with the correctness arguments in each linked document.

## Performance evidence

[Benchmark driver](<../96-Local Testing/05-Mathematics/90-foundations_benchmark.py>), [C++ workload](<../96-Local Testing/05-Mathematics/90-foundations_benchmark.cpp>) and [record](<../96-Local Testing/05-Mathematics/90-foundations_benchmark.json>) hold CPU, compiler, flags, workloads, memory models, one warmup and five samples with medians. Inputs are deterministic, outputs are checked outside the timed region, and no timing gate is applied. The re-audit added a `[10^16 - 10^5, 10^16)` stream and a shared-base materialization workload. The before/after comparison of the segmented-sieve fix is in [95-segmentedsieve.md](95-segmentedsieve.md). It shows Odd at `10^12` (block 32768) going from 63.97 to 21.39 ms and Wheel30 at `10^16` from 396.49 to 84.05 ms. Plain improved or held on every workload. Dense tables through 2,000,000 recorded 7.10 ms packed-bit reference, 5.77 ms byte Eratosthenes and 7.78 ms linear SPF in the same run. No Barrett/Montgomery or ISA-specific code is used. `fibSearch` makes at most `log_phi(n + 1) + 1` evaluations, against `2 * ceil(log2(n))` for `ternSearch`; that count is its cost evidence.

## API migration and remaining scope

The segmented-sieve constructor now consistently means **`[L,R)`**. Use `SegmentedSieve::fromClosed` for old closed intervals; it rejects an unrepresentable exclusive maximum endpoint. Divisor sums now use `lll`. There were no active dependent call sites to migrate. Legacy originals remain untouched in `OLD` and `97-Legacy`.

`ModFac` remains available. Its empty with-replacement count is corrected to `combiWR(0,0)==1`. Factorial tables require a prime modulus and `n<mod`; large-index Lucas-theorem/composite counting remains in MA26, while Lucas *numbers* are implemented here. Inverse tables/CRT and general primality/factorization remain with their existing later owners. Enormous segmented queries still require square-root base-prime storage; documentation reports that cost and the tested high-endpoint limits.

Handoffs from the research sweep (not P012 work): `firstModInRange` to `18-floorsum.hpp`, the completely multiplicative power table to `08-multiplicative_functions.hpp`. There is no unfinished owned P012 feature or verification gap. Separate planned inventory families remain planned. No online submission or online-acceptance claim was made.
